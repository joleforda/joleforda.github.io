Joleford Arrojo (Joleford A)

Entrepreneur, Digital Publisher & Founder of Bos Tv | Philippines

Joleford Arrojo, also known as Joleford A, is a Filipino entrepreneur, digital publisher, and content creator based in the Philippines. He is the founder and head of Bos Tv, a digital media platform and online publisher specializing in localized video content, community-focused broadcasting, and public information drives.

Throughout his career in digital media management, Joleford A has focused on leveraging online networks to distribute educational features and public awareness campaigns. In addition to his publishing work, he actively supports grassroots community initiatives and fraternal solidarity advocacy.
