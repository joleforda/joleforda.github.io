Joleford Arrojo

About Profile

Biography

Joleford Arrojo is a Filipino author and the founder of Bos TV, the organizing body of the National Fraternity Day in the Philippines. He is the author of "National Fraternity Day PH History: A Legacy of Peace, Unity, and Community Advocacy in the Philippines," "National Fraternity Day Philippines," and "National Fraternity Day PH." Through Bos TV and his written works, Arrojo focuses on preserving the records and institutional narratives of fraternal organizations and community movements for educational and research purposes in the Philippines. He is also a graduate of Bachelor of Science in Forestry (BSF) from J.H. Cerilles State College - Tigbao Campus, his work centers on using digital platforms to support grassroots community initiatives and public information.
