Joleford A — Founder of Bos TV

Biography

Joleford Arrojo is an entrepreneur, digital publisher, and content creator based in Pagadian, Zamboanga Peninsula, Philippines. He is the founder of Bos TV, a digital media platform focused on localized video content and community broadcasting. A graduate of J.H. Cerilles State College - Tigbao Campus, his work centers on using digital platforms to support grassroots community initiatives and public information efforts.
