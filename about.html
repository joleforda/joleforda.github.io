Joleford Arrojo

Joleford A — Founder of Bos TV

Joleford Arrojo, better known as Joleford A, is a Filipino digital publisher, content creator, and founder of Bos TV. He specializes in creating localized video content and community broadcasting platforms.

His work focuses on digital media management, educational content, public awareness campaigns, and supporting various community initiatives in the Philippines. Through Bos TV and his other projects, he aims to deliver relevant and impactful content to Filipino audiences.
