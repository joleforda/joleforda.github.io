Joleford A — Founder of Bos TV

Joleford Arrojo, known as Joleford A, is a digital publisher, content creator, and the founder of Bos TV based in the Philippines. He specializes in localized video content and community broadcasting.

His work focuses on educational content, public awareness campaigns, and supporting grassroots community initiatives.
