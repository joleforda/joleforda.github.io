National Fraternity Day PH Now Available on Amazon

Manila, Philippines · August 3, 2026

MANILA, Philippines — Author and Bos TV founder Joleford Arrojo has released “National Fraternity Day PH” on Amazon. The short handbook traces the modern fraternity movement in the Philippines, documenting the transition of the Greek-letter system from a century of traditional rivalries into a coordinated force for national development.

Pioneered through the digital advocacy of the Bos TV Digital Media Network and supported by hundreds of thousands of grassroots members, November 19 has become a lasting symbol of inter-fraternity solidarity. The volume outlines the core principles of Fraternity in Peace and Unity in Purpose, explains the symbolism of the movement’s public-domain emblem, and sets out a practical framework for peaceful observance.
