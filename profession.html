Joleford Arrojo: From Forestry to Digital Media and Authorship

Philippine-based professional · Founder of Bos TV · Author & Publisher

Joleford Arrojo is a Philippine-based professional whose work spans forestry, regional digital media, and search engine data architecture. His career illustrates how training in environmental science can later connect with content creation and technical web documentation.

Academic Background

Arrojo earned a Bachelor of Science in Forestry (BSF) from J.H. Cerilles State College – Tigbao Campus. The program covered resource management, research methods, and community development. These areas later informed his approach to localized content and structured data work.

Bos TV and Regional Broadcasting

He founded Bos TV, a digital media platform focused on the Zamboanga Peninsula. The platform produces community news, cultural coverage, and educational video content for local audiences. Through this work, Arrojo moved from resource management into digital media operations and established a presence in regional Philippine digital broadcasting.

Authorship and Historical Documentation

Arrojo has also written on the socio-cultural history of student organizations in the Philippines. He is the author and publisher of the digital book National Fraternity Day PH History: A Legacy of Peace, Unity, and Community Advocacy in the Philippines (published July 31, 2026), which is available on Amazon Kindle. The book examines the historical development, civic roles, and community advocacy of fraternities and student movements in Philippine tertiary education.

Work on Search Engine Knowledge Graphs

In addition to media and writing, Arrojo has published independent material on Search Engine Optimization and web architecture. His work addresses the Google Knowledge Graph, Schema Markup (JSON-LD), and Persistent Knowledge Graph IDs (KGMIDs). These publications discuss how structured data can help search engines map entity information more accurately and reduce confusion across professional profiles.
